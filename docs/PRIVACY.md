# Jarlheim — Privacy policy (short form)

Jarlheim is a free game in development.

- **Offline play:** your hold is saved only on your own device (IndexedDB).
- **Signing in** (Google, username and password, or guest) stores only what the game needs online: your player name, your place and power on the realm map, a snapshot of your defences for player battles, your chat messages and reactions, friends, alliance membership and support tickets. Google sign-in shares your Google account id and email with our server provider (Supabase); we never see your Google password.
- **No selling, no ads, no tracking** of you outside the game.
- **Moderation:** reported chat messages may be read by the team; a word filter runs on the server.
- **Deleting:** Settings → Careful → *Delete my account* removes your account and online data. *Delete this character* clears the hold on your device.
- **Contact:** Settings → Customer service, inside the game.

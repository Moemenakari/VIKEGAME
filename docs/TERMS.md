1. Play fair: no cheats, bots, exploits, scams, hate or harassment. Be respectful in chat.
2. Gold is earned by playing. There are no payments in this version (see MONETIZATION.md; payments come only at M11, with their own terms).
3. Accounts that break these rules may be muted, reset or removed.
4. The game is provided as it is while it is being built. Features, balance and saves may change or reset.
5. History in the game is told in our own words from public-domain sources (see HISTORY_BIBLE.md); legends are marked as legends.
